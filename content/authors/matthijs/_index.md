Matthijs is a theoretical physicist specialising in modified theories of gravity, modelling and research software engineering.

As part of the group, he works on extending [Rubin](https://rubinobservatory.org/) photometry to be suitable for joint Rubin-[Roman](https://roman.gsfc.nasa.gov/) modelling.

Previously, he worked on high-resolution imaging pipelines for the [International Low-Frequency ARray (LOFAR) Telescope](https://www.astron.nl/telescopes/lofar/) and, before that, extended canonical quantisation methods to study the quantum effects of modified theories of gravity in the (very) early universe.
